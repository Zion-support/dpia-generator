## What it does
Workflow: processing-context intake, necessity & proportionality analysis, risk scoring, mitigation library, DPO review and sign-off trail.

Key capabilities:
- Continuous monitoring with explainable AI findings and risk impact
- One-click playbooks routed to your privacy/GRC stack
- Weekly compliance digest with assessment totals
- Connects to the rest of the Zion App Network for end-to-end compliance operations
